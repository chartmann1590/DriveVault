DriveVault Dashcam turns your Android phone into a privacy-first dashcam. No accounts, no cloud uploads, no tracking — your data stays on your device unless you choose to share.

RECORDING
- Continuous loop recording in 60-second segments
- Rear, front, and dual-camera modes (concurrent CameraX)
- Background recording via foreground service with notification controls (stop, lock clip)
- Auto-deletes oldest unlocked clips when storage is full

GPS & TELEMETRY
- Real-time speed, heading, and coordinates overlay
- Compass direction display
- OSM-based mini map — no API keys needed
- Full telemetry recording per clip (location samples, heading, snapshots)

CLIP MANAGEMENT
- Browse, search, and filter your clip library (by camera mode, time range, lock status)
- Lock important clips to prevent auto-deletion
- Clip detail view with ExoPlayer video playback and full metadata

EXPORT & SHARING
- Export raw video, metadata JSON, or GPX route files
- Location redaction for privacy-preserving exports
- Share via Android share sheet with trimmed 50MB limit

IMMICH INTEGRATION (Optional)
- Sync clips to your self-hosted Immich server
- Encrypted API key storage
- Configurable sync rules (Wi-Fi only, charging only)

ANONYMOUS CLIP SHARING (Optional)
- Share clips via time-limited links (24-hour expiry)
- No account required to view shared clips
- All sharing is opt-in and off by default

AI DETECTION (Optional)
- Real-time vehicle and person detection via MLKit
- Bounding box overlay on camera preview
- On-device only — no cloud inference

PRIVACY
- No cloud uploads by default
- No accounts or sign-up required
- No ads, no data selling
- Encrypted storage for sensitive settings

Perfect for daily commuters, rideshare drivers, and road trip enthusiasts who value their privacy.
