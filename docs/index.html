DriveVault is a privacy-first dashcam app for Android. Record with GPS overlays, dual-camera support, and total control over your footage.

Open Source Android 8.0+ No Cloud Required

Everything you need

A full-featured dashcam that respects your privacy

🎥

Loop Recording

Records in 60-second segments and auto-deletes the oldest unlocked clips when storage fills up. Never run out of space.

📡

Dual Camera

Simultaneously record from front and rear cameras on supported devices. Capture both perspectives of your drive.

📍

GPS Overlay

Real-time speed, coordinates, and compass heading overlaid on the camera preview with an OSM mini map.

🔒

Clip Locking

Lock important clips to protect them from auto-deletion. Your critical footage is always safe.

📦

Export & Share

Export raw video, metadata JSON, or GPX route files. Share via Android share sheet or save to gallery.

☁️

Immich Sync

Optional sync to your own self-hosted Immich server. Your clips, your infrastructure, your rules.

Privacy by default

Your data never leaves your device unless you say so

🔒

No cloud uploads

Videos, GPS, and metadata stay on your device. No automatic uploads ever.

👤

No account required

Install, grant permissions, and start recording. No sign-up or login.

👁‍️

No ads or tracking

Zero ads, zero analytics tracking of your personal data or location history.

🔐

Encrypted storage

API keys and sensitive settings are stored using Android encrypted preferences.
